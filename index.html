Out of Control

This game is one of the first I've ever created. The key idea is that when the sphere at the bottom reaches a block with an arrow on it, the ship the player controls will be launched in the direction of that arrow. This creates a timing and puzzle challenge where the player must take into account this uncontrollable movements while navigating the level.
